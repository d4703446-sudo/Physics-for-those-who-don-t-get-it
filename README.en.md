## ✨ Why it's convenient

Each formula is an expandable card:

> **v = S / t**
>
> 🧠 *In simple terms* · 📌 *Symbols and units* · ✏️ *Example* · ⚠️ *Common mistake* · ↳ *Derivation* · 🧮 *Calculator*

Formulas are rendered via KaTeX and look like a real textbook. Advanced topics are marked as **adv.** to not scare beginners.

## 🚀 Quick Start

**Just open:** download `index.html` and double-click the file.

<br>

<details>
<summary><b>Technical Details</b></summary>

<br>

- Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No build step, no frameworks.
- The page is rendered from the `SECTIONS` array on load.
- Math: [KaTeX](https://katex.org/) via CDN (falls back to TeX text offline).
- Fonts: Inter and JetBrains Mono (falls back to system fonts offline).
- Favorites, theme, and open cards are saved in `localStorage`.
- Calculators use g = 9.8 m/s² (often rounded to 10 in school problems).

</details>

<br>

## ⚠️ Important

This is an educational reference. Constants and table values are rounded to high school precision. If in doubt, double-check with your textbook.
